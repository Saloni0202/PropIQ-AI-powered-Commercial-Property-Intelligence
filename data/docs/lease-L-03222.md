# Lease Abstract L-03222 - Redwood Medical Group at Crescent Medical Plaza
Property: Crescent Medical Plaza | Market: Chicago | Tenant: Redwood Medical Group | Type: Medical Office Class C

## Premises and Term
Redwood Medical Group (Health Care) leases 4,560 SF at Crescent Medical Plaza in Chicago, IL. The lease commenced on 2025-09-10 and expires on 2028-09-09, a term of 3 years. The lease structure is Full Service Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $31.34 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 2 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
