# Market Report Q3 2026 - New York
Market: New York | Period: Q3 2026 | Source: PropIQ research (synthetic)

## Market Overview
The New York market closed September 2026 with an average asking rent of $51.28 per SF per year, up 3.7% year over year. The vacancy rate was 13.8%, down 1.0 percentage points from a year earlier.

## Demand Drivers
Leasing demand in New York is led by financial services, media and law firms, with a flight-to-quality bias towards amenitised Class A towers. Trailing twelve-month net absorption was 1,334 thousand SF.

## Rents by Segment
Among property types, Medical Office commands the highest average rent in New York at $88.06 per SF. Class A office averages $90.01 per SF versus $49.21 per SF for Class C office, a premium of 83%.

## Outlook and Risks
We expect modest rent growth in New York over the next twelve months. Key risks are hybrid-work driven space reductions, new supply deliveries and tenant credit quality. Landlords are offering concessions such as free rent and tenant improvement allowances to secure renewals.
