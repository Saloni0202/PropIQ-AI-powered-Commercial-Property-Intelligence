Leasing demand in San Francisco is led by AI and software start-ups slowly re-leasing space, while legacy tech footprints keep shrinking. Trailing twelve-month net absorption was 745 thousand SF.

## Outlook and Risks
We expect flat to softening rents in San Francisco over the next twelve months. Key risks are hybrid-work driven space reductions, new supply deliveries and tenant credit quality. Landlords are offering concessions such as free rent and tenant improvement allowances to secure renewals.
