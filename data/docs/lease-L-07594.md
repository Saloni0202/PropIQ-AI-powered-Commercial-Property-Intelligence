# Lease Abstract L-07594 - Ironwood Manufacturing at Crescent Industrial Park
Property: Crescent Industrial Park | Market: Boston | Tenant: Ironwood Manufacturing | Type: Industrial Class C

## Premises and Term
Ironwood Manufacturing (Manufacturing) leases 60,480 SF at Crescent Industrial Park in Boston, MA. The lease commenced on 2022-06-26 and expires on 2027-06-25, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $7.98 per SF per year. Rent escalates 3.5% annually. The tenant received 0 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $40 per SF. The security deposit equals 2 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
