# Lease Abstract L-00340 - Harbor Cafe at Silverline Commons
Property: Silverline Commons | Market: Charlotte | Tenant: Harbor Cafe | Type: Retail Class A

## Premises and Term
Harbor Cafe (Retail & Consumer) leases 14,330 SF at Silverline Commons in Charlotte, NC. The lease commenced on 2019-01-07 and expires on 2029-01-04, a term of 10 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $31.66 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 3 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
