# Lease Abstract L-06480 - Summit Medical Group at Beacon Clinic Center
Property: Beacon Clinic Center | Market: San Diego | Tenant: Summit Medical Group | Type: Medical Office Class A

## Premises and Term
Summit Medical Group (Health Care) leases 6,020 SF at Beacon Clinic Center in San Diego, CA. The lease commenced on 2022-12-07 and expires on 2027-12-06, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is Modified Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $51.62 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $40 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
