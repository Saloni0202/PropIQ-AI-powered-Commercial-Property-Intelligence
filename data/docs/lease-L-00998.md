# Lease Abstract L-00998 - Granite Labs at Eastgate Village
Property: Eastgate Village | Market: Chicago | Tenant: Granite Labs | Type: Retail Class B

## Premises and Term
Granite Labs (Technology) leases 8,340 SF at Eastgate Village in Chicago, IL. The lease commenced on 2018-11-15 and expires on 2028-11-12, a term of 10 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $39.73 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
