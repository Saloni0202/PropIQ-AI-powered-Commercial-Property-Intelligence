# Lease Abstract L-02737 - Summit Foods at Summit Crossing
Property: Summit Crossing | Market: New York | Tenant: Summit Foods | Type: Retail Class B

## Premises and Term
Summit Foods (Retail & Consumer) leases 33,060 SF at Summit Crossing in New York, NY. The lease commenced on 2024-01-03 and expires on 2029-01-01, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $52.63 per SF per year. Rent escalates 3.0% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $80 per SF. The security deposit equals 2 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
