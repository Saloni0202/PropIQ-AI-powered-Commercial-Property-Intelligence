# Lease Abstract L-07101 - Blue Harbor Consulting at Riverside Building
Property: Riverside Building | Market: Miami | Tenant: Blue Harbor Consulting | Type: Office Class B

## Premises and Term
Blue Harbor Consulting (Professional Services) leases 660 SF at Riverside Building in Miami, FL. The lease commenced on 2024-08-22 and expires on 2031-08-21, a term of 7 years. The lease structure is Full Service Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $41.63 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $20 per SF. The security deposit equals 3 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
