# Lease Abstract L-03468 - Juniper Asset Management at Eastgate Place
Property: Eastgate Place | Market: Salt Lake City | Tenant: Juniper Asset Management | Type: Office Class C

## Premises and Term
Juniper Asset Management (Financial Services) leases 2,410 SF at Eastgate Place in Salt Lake City, UT. The lease commenced on 2023-01-30 and expires on 2028-01-29, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is Modified Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $12.24 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 0 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $40 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
