# Lease Abstract L-04156 - Redwood Apparel at Granite Marketplace
Property: Granite Marketplace | Market: San Francisco | Tenant: Redwood Apparel | Type: Retail Class A

## Premises and Term
Redwood Apparel (Retail & Consumer) leases 11,090 SF at Granite Marketplace in San Francisco, CA. The lease commenced on 2025-12-01 and expires on 2035-11-29, a term of 10 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $64.65 per SF per year. Rent escalates 3.5% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $40 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
