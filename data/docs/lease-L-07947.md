# Lease Abstract L-07947 - Evergreen Health at Apex Promenade
Property: Apex Promenade | Market: New York | Tenant: Evergreen Health | Type: Retail Class C

## Premises and Term
Evergreen Health (Health Care) leases 18,410 SF at Apex Promenade in New York, NY. The lease commenced on 2022-08-15 and expires on 2027-08-14, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $52.64 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
