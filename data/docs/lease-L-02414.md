# Lease Abstract L-02414 - Vertex Clinic at Pinnacle Clinic Center
Property: Pinnacle Clinic Center | Market: Boston | Tenant: Vertex Clinic | Type: Medical Office Class B

## Premises and Term
Vertex Clinic (Health Care) leases 2,850 SF at Pinnacle Clinic Center in Boston, MA. The lease commenced on 2025-07-30 and expires on 2032-07-28, a term of 7 years. The lease structure is Modified Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $58.74 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $0 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
