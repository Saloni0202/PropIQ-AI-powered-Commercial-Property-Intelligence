# Lease Abstract L-01969 - Ironwood Media at Granite Flex Center
Property: Granite Flex Center | Market: Salt Lake City | Tenant: Ironwood Media | Type: Flex Class B

## Premises and Term
Ironwood Media (Media & Publishing) leases 11,290 SF at Granite Flex Center in Salt Lake City, UT. The lease commenced on 2024-11-04 and expires on 2029-11-03, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $11.67 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 6 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 3 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: nan. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
