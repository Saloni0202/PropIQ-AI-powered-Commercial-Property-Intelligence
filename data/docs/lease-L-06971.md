# Lease Abstract L-06971 - Beacon Media at Pinnacle Center
Property: Pinnacle Center | Market: Denver | Tenant: Beacon Media | Type: Office Class B

## Premises and Term
Beacon Media (Media & Publishing) leases 3,400 SF at Pinnacle Center in Denver, CO. The lease commenced on 2025-07-29 and expires on 2028-07-28, a term of 3 years. The lease structure is Modified Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $22.37 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $40 per SF. The security deposit equals 3 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
