# Lease Abstract L-05325 - Orchid Cafe at Eastgate Promenade
Property: Eastgate Promenade | Market: Salt Lake City | Tenant: Orchid Cafe | Type: Retail Class C

## Premises and Term
Orchid Cafe (Retail & Consumer) leases 6,650 SF at Eastgate Promenade in Salt Lake City, UT. The lease commenced on 2023-01-24 and expires on 2028-01-23, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $9.75 per SF per year. Rent escalates 3.0% annually. The tenant received 0 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 2 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Mutual termination after year 7 with 12 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
