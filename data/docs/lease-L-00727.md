# Lease Abstract L-00727 - Vertex Medical Group at Cedar Clinic Center
Property: Cedar Clinic Center | Market: Charlotte | Tenant: Vertex Medical Group | Type: Medical Office Class B

## Premises and Term
Vertex Medical Group (Health Care) leases 9,250 SF at Cedar Clinic Center in Charlotte, NC. The lease commenced on 2021-09-12 and expires on 2028-09-10, a term of 7 years. The lease structure is Full Service Gross.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $33.42 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.5% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 1 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 1 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
