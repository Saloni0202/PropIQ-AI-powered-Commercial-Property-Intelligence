# Lease Abstract L-00319 - Granite Ventures at Eastgate Business Park
Property: Eastgate Business Park | Market: Washington DC | Tenant: Granite Ventures | Type: Flex Class B

## Premises and Term
Granite Ventures (Other) leases 20,860 SF at Eastgate Business Park in Washington DC, DC. The lease commenced on 2022-06-09 and expires on 2032-06-06, a term of 10 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations
Current base rent is $27.01 per SF per year. Rent escalates 2.0% annually. The tenant received 2 months of free rent and a tenant improvement allowance of $60 per SF. The security deposit equals 3 months of rent.

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: Tenant may terminate after year 5 with 9 months notice. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
