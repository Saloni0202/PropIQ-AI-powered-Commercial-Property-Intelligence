# Lease Abstract L-02786 - Redwood Ventures at Capitol Shops
Property: Capitol Shops | Market: Washington DC | Tenant: Redwood Ventures | Type: Retail Class B

## Premises and Term
Redwood Ventures (Other) leases 6,080 SF at Capitol Shops in Washington DC, DC. The lease commenced on 2023-10-08 and expires on 2028-10-06, a term of 5 years. The lease structure is NNN.

## Rent and Escalations

## Options and Termination Rights
Renewal option: 2 x 5 years. Termination right: nan. Assignment and subletting require landlord consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.
