The Washington DC market closed September 2026 with an average asking rent of $29.31 per SF per year, up 1.7% year over year. The vacancy rate was 16.4%, down 0.6 percentage points from a year earlier.

Leasing demand in Washington DC is led by federal agencies, defence contractors and associations, with hybrid work limiting demand. Trailing twelve-month net absorption was 596 thousand SF.

## Outlook and Risks
We expect modest rent growth in Washington DC over the next twelve months. Key risks are hybrid-work driven space reductions, new supply deliveries and tenant credit quality. Landlords are offering concessions such as free rent and tenant improvement allowances to secure renewals.
