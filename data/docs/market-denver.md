Leasing demand in Denver is led by energy transition, aerospace and outdoor-industry firms. Trailing twelve-month net absorption was 763 thousand SF.

We expect flat to softening rents in Denver over the next twelve months. Key risks are hybrid-work driven space reductions, new supply deliveries and tenant credit quality. Landlords are offering concessions such as free rent and tenant improvement allowances to secure renewals.
